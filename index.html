The Dramatized Negative

A valence and vividness experiment with local language models

I tested how instruct-tuned models describe positive and negative experiences. The clearest result was a repeatable tendency to dramatize negative human conflict; the explanations I proposed for other patterns did not survive their pre-registered tests.
